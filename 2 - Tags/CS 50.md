
CS50 is Harvard University's introductory computer science course, formally known as **Computer Science 50** or **Introduction to Computer Science** . It is one of Harvard's most popular and well-known courses, designed for both computer science majors and non-majors, including those with no prior programming experience .

### Key Aspects of CS50

*   **Course Content**: It covers foundational computer science concepts such as **abstraction, algorithms, data structures, resource management, security, software engineering, and web development** . The course emphasizes learning how to solve problems algorithmically and think computationally, rather than just teaching a single programming language .
*   **Programming Languages**: Students are introduced to a range of languages, typically starting with **C** to build a foundational understanding of how computers work, then moving to higher-level languages like **Python, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS** .
*   **Teaching Style**: Led by Harvard Professor **David J. Malan**, the course is known for its engaging and dynamic lectures. Malan famously uses props and demonstrations—like tearing a phone book to illustrate binary search—to make complex concepts accessible .
*   **Online Availability**: CS50 is available for free to learners worldwide through the **edX platform** as **CS50x**. You can audit the course for free or pay for a verified certificate . It is also shared with **Yale University** via video streaming .
