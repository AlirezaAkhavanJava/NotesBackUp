
Here's a table of the main **network types**, organized roughly from smallest to largest coverage area.

| Network Type | Full Name | Coverage Area | Simple Explanation |
|--------------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **PAN** | Personal Area Network | ~1–10 meters | Connects your personal devices, like your phone to earbuds or a smartwatch. Think Bluetooth. |
| **LAN** | Local Area Network | A room, home, office, or building | Connects devices in one place, like your home Wi-Fi or a school computer lab. |
| **WLAN** | Wireless Local Area Network | Same as LAN, but wireless | A LAN that uses Wi-Fi instead of cables. Your home Wi-Fi is a WLAN. |
| **CAN** | Campus Area Network | A campus or group of nearby buildings | Connects multiple LANs across a university or corporate campus. |
| **MAN** | Metropolitan Area Network | A city or large town | Covers a city — like a cable TV network or a city-wide ISP network. |
| **WAN** | Wide Area Network | A country, continent, or the world | Connects networks over huge distances. The Internet is the biggest WAN. |
| **SAN** | Storage Area Network | A data center | A specialized network that connects servers to storage devices at high speed. |
| **VPN** | Virtual Private Network | Anywhere (over the Internet) | A secure tunnel over the Internet that makes you appear on another network, protecting your privacy. |
| **EPN** | Enterprise Private Network | A whole organization | A private network built by a company to connect all its offices and resources securely. |
| **GAN** | Global Area Network | Worldwide | A network spanning the globe, often via satellites — like a multinational company's network. |
| **BAN** | Body Area Network | On or inside a person's body | Wearable tech like fitness trackers or medical implants communicating wirelessly. |
| **HAN** | Home Area Network | A single home | Connects all devices in your home — computers, smart TVs, phones, smart home gadgets. |

---

## Quick Grouping by Size

- **Tiny (personal):** PAN, BAN
- **Local:** LAN, WLAN, HAN
- **Medium:** CAN, MAN
- **Large:** WAN, GAN, EPN
- **Special purpose:** SAN (storage), VPN (secure remote access)

---

## Simple Rule of Thumb

- **PAN/BAN** → around *you*
- **LAN/WLAN/HAN** → around *one building*
- **CAN/MAN** → around *a campus or city*
- **WAN/GAN** → around *the world*

The key difference between them is mostly **how far they reach** and **who owns/uses them**.
