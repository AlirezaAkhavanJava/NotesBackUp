In networking, **PAN** stands for **Personal Area Network**.

## Definition
A **Personal Area Network (PAN)** is a network that connects electronic devices within a very short range, typically around a single person's workspace (usually within about 10 meters / 30 feet).

## Key Characteristics
- **Short range** – usually a few meters to ~10 meters
- **Personal scope** – centered on one individual
- **Low power** – designed for battery-efficient devices
- **Small number of devices** – phones, laptops, earbuds, wearables, etc.

## Types of PAN

**1. Wireless PAN (WPAN)**
Uses wireless technologies such as:
- **Bluetooth** – most common (e.g., phone to earbuds, keyboard, smartwatch)
- **Infrared (IrDA)** – older, line-of-sight
- **Zigbee / Z-Wave** – smart home devices
- **NFC** – very short range (a few centimeters)

**2. Wired PAN**
Uses physical connections such as:
- **USB** – connecting a phone or peripheral to a computer
- **FireWire / Thunderbolt** – high-speed short-range connections

## Common Examples
- Smartphone connected to wireless earbuds
- Laptop connected to a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard
- Smartwatch syncing with a phone
- Fitness tracker sending data to an app

## PAN vs. Other Networks
| Network | Range | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| PAN | ~10 m | Phone ↔ earbuds |
| LAN | Building/campus | Home Wi-Fi |
| MAN | City | Cable TV network |
| WAN | Country/global | The Internet |

In short, a **PAN** is the smallest and most personal type of network, linking devices you use directly around yourself.
