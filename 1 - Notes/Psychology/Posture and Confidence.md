## Psychological Research on Posture and Confidence

### 1. **Improved Mood, Higher Self-Esteem, Reduced Stress**

A controlled study with 74 participants found that those who sat upright during stress reported better mood, higher self-esteem, and less fear compared to those who slouched. [SELF](https://www.self.com/story/one-easy-fix-will-improve-self-esteem?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[TIME](https://time.com/3394589/slumping-makes-you-sad/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 2. **Posture Affects Mood, Confidence, and Performance**

A 2017 study on people with mild to moderate depression showed sitting upright increased positive emotions, reduced fatigue, and improved speech fluency—demonstrating posture’s emotional impact. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/traversmark/2024/09/24/3-unexpected-benefits-of-good-posture-on-mental-health-by-a-psychologist/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

Another study found that even brief changes in posture—like expanding the chest or standing straight—boosted positive feelings, while slouched positions reduced mood and confidence. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10237498/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 3. **Embodied Cognition: Body Posture Influences Emotions**

Modern theory of embodied cognition shows how our posture can directly alter our emotional state. Slumped posture is associated with sadness and negative thinking, whereas open, upright posture ties into confidence and positive mood. [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/intl/blog/brain-reboot/202408/your-physical-posture-could-change-your-mood?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 4. **More Alertness, Formality, or Self-Impression**

In one qualitative study, people described upright posture as leading to feelings of alertness and formality (“wanting to make a good impression”), while slumping linked to worry and low prestige. [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-8611/5/2/30?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 5. **Posture Validates Our Thoughts**

Research from Ohio State University showed that when individuals sat upright, they were more confident in their own thoughts (whether positive or negative). Slouched individuals were less affected by their own thoughts. [Ohio State News](https://news.osu.edu/study--body-posture-affects-confidence-in-your-own-thoughts/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 6. **The ‘Power Posing’ Debate**

Early studies suggested "power posing" (expansive, dominant poses) could boost confidence, lower stress hormones, and even influence hormones like testosterone. [Glamour](https://www.glamour.com/story/feeling-depressed-maybe-you-sh?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[TIME](https://time.com/3007520/be-more-powerful/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[Wikipedia+1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Cuddy?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

However, more rigorous later studies have questioned these findings, showing that expansive poses may not consistently produce hormonal changes. What is better supported is that slumping _reduces_ mood and performance—suggesting the real impact is about avoiding a contractive posture rather than "power posing" per se.
