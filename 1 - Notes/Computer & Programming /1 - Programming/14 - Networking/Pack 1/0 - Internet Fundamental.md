1. **You → ISP**  
    Your computer/phone connects to your **ISP** (via Wi-Fi, cable, fiber, or mobile data).
    
2. **ISP → Internet**  
    The ISP sends your request (like opening Google) into the big network of networks we call the **Internet**.
    
3. **DNS = Address Book**  
    When you type `google.com`, a system called **DNS** finds the exact server’s IP address (like looking up a phone number).
    
4. **Servers Answer Back**  
    Your request reaches Google’s server, it prepares a response (like the search page), and sends it back through the same path.
    
5. **Data Travels in Packets**  
    Information moves as tiny chunks called **packets**. They fly through routers and switches worldwide until they reach you.
    
6. **You See the Result**  
    Your browser reassembles the packets and shows you the website.
    

👉 In short:  
**Device → ISP → Internet routers → Website’s server → Back to you**
