

Wrath is no companion of mine, but a ravenous inferno that devours the very hand that kindles it.

Vengeance is no instrument of justice, but a sepulcher for two, bearing my name foremost.

Lust is no wellspring of fulfillment, but an insatiable abyss that devours its own sustained.

Envy is no measure of another’s wisdom, but a canker in the soul that feaster upon its own host.

Obstinacy is no strength of conviction, but a sepulcher wherein I entomb my reason and christen its silence virtue.

Self-pity is no balm for my afflictions, but a throne of ashes upon which I reign sovereign over my own desolation.

Sloth is no sanctuary of mine, but a quiet rot that feaster on my years while I call it rest.

Procrastination is no exercise of patience, but the theft of today by a tomorrow that may never come.  

Distraction is no respite for the mind, but the surrender of my attention to the inconsequential.  

Wastefulness is no freedom of choice, but the squandering of one's finite existence.  

Impatience is no servant of ambition, but the enslavement of reason to time.  

Reverie is no consummation of ambition, but a banquet of phantoms whilst reality remains famished.

Perfectionism is no pursuit of excellence, but the prison of my own expectations.  

Adversity is no justification for surrender, but the crucible of perseverance.  

Memories are no chains of the past, but the foundations of wisdom.

Cowardice is no protection from suffering, but the surrender of my courage.  

Timidity is no safeguard of my dignity, but the subjugation of my will to the judgement of others.  

Silence in the face of truth is no preservation of peace, but a renunciation of my integrity.  

Self-abasement is no avenue to approbation, but an invitation to derision.  

Obsequiousness is no avenue to affection, but the prostration of my dignity beneath another's heel.

Loquacity is no instrument of wit, but the architect of my own derision.

Imitation is no path to wisdom, but the abandonment of my own discernment.  

Credulity is no mark of a noble heart, but my complicity in my own deception.

Speech is no foe of the wise, but a blade that demands its proper hour.  

Silence is no virtue in itself, but a tool I must learn when to lay down.

Self-reproach is no penance, but a scourge that breeds the idleness it condemns.


Silence is my ally; for he who cannot govern his tongue shall seldom command the respect of others.

He who bridles not his tongue shall find folly in his words; wisdom dweller in the mouth of the restrained.

Merit requires no proclamation; let my deeds speak where my tongue would boast.

He who entrusts his faith to every tongue shall soon find himself deceived by many.
